secret-rs

Share safely.

Create a one-time secret link with client-side encryption. The browser generates the key, encrypts the secret locally, and sends only ciphertext and a nonce to the server. The key stays in the # fragment, the secret can be read once, and nothing can be recovered after read, expiration, or early deletion.
