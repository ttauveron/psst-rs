psst

Share a secret.

psst creates one-time secret links with client-side encryption. Your browser generates the key, encrypts the secret locally, and sends only ciphertext plus a nonce to the server. The key stays in the # fragment, the secret can be read once, and nothing can be recovered after read, expiration, or early deletion.
